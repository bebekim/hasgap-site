Hasgap Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Hasgap keeps what you log on your iPhone. We don't run a server, you don't make an account, and nothing you log is sent to us.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter: meals, stress, runs and workouts, sleep, waist measurements, urges and how you rode them out, notes, photos and short video clips. It is stored in the app's own folder on your iPhone. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or a computer, that backup can include it, under Apple's or your computer's terms.

Deleting an entry removes it from the app. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

What we collect

Nothing. Hasgap has no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking, and it doesn't share data with anyone.

Payments

Membership is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and tells the app only whether you are a member and until when. We never see your name, card or Apple Account details. See Apple's privacy policy.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose Moment, to record a 2-second silent clip, and iOS asks your permission first. A photo you pick is copied into the app. Both stay on your phone.

Children

Hasgap is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if we add anonymous usage counts, we will update this page and its date before the change ships.
